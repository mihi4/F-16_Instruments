### RWR

The RWR uses a 2.8 round display (search around on Ali, at 2026-05 it goes for around 50,- including the driver board) and a simple rotary encoder.  
To connect Top Front and Top Back 2.5mm heat inserts and 2.5x6mm countersink screws are used.
Everything else is self explanatory, I think.  
You can use any method to output the pins of the rotary encoder, I chose a DB9 connector, the counterpart will go to a 23017 I/O-Extender on the TWP, I guess.
